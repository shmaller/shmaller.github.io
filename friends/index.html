The icons below are links to some cool webrings that I'm lucky to be a member of. A webring is an old-school internet connection tool, where each member of the ring links to the next member in the "chain". It gives you neighbors in the digital space! Like a little community on the web.

The first webring, XXIIVV, is a chain of artists' portfolio sites. Check it out! There are a ton of creative and beautiful websites in there, designed by real people to showcase their work.

The second is the NoAI webring, made up of websites built by hand, with no AI assistance and in resistance to the AI slopification of the web.
